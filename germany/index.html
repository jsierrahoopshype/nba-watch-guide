How to watch the NBA in Germany in 2026-27

Where to watch

In Germany, NBA games are on Sky Deutschland / WOW and Prime Video, and every game is live on NBA League Pass.

These details come from 2026 news reports rather than an official 2026-27 source; check before you buy.

Sky Deutschland / WOW

What it shows: Up to six regular-season games a week on average, all five Christmas Day games, selected MLK Day games, All-Star Weekend, selected first- and second-round playoff games, and all Conference Finals and NBA Finals games live or as replays.

Commentary: US original broadcast, with German commentary on selected games
