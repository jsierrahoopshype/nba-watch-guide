How to watch the NBA in France in 2026-27

Where to watch

In France, NBA games are on beIN SPORTS and Prime Video, and every game is live on NBA League Pass.

These details come from 2026 news reports rather than an official 2026-27 source; check before you buy.

beIN SPORTS

What it shows: Up to nine regular-season games a week, Christmas Day, MLK Day, All-Star Weekend, a selection of playoff games, a Conference Final and the NBA Finals in alternate years, plus next-day replays at noon.

Commentary: French

beIN SPORTS
No commitment. Also sold through Orange, SFR, Bouygues, Free and Canal+.
€15 a month

Prime Video

Included with an Amazon Prime membership in all five countries. 67 regular-season games announced for 2026-27 plus 20 more European-primetime games to be announced, all NBA Cup knockout games, the full Play-In, selected early playoff games and the Eastern Conference Finals exclusively.

Commentary: French (confirmed for 2025-26; 2026-27 not yet confirmed), with a US commentary option

Price not confirmed
