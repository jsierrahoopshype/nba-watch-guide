Questions people ask

Why can I not watch some games on NBA League Pass?
League Pass has blackouts. These are the rules that apply: Nationally broadcast games cannot be watched live on League Pass and become available at 6am ET the next day. Games televised locally in your area cannot be watched live on League Pass and become available three days after the game.
Which NBA games can I watch for free?
Games on a free over-the-air channel need only an antenna. Right now that covers: ABC (free over the air), NBC (free over the air).
What is the difference between in-market and out-of-market?
In-market means you live in the area a team's local broadcaster covers. Those local games are on that broadcaster and are blacked out on League Pass. Out-of-market means you live anywhere else, so the local broadcast is not available to you but League Pass is.
How current are these prices?
Each service carries the date its price was last checked. Anything without a confirmed price shows as not confirmed instead of a number.
