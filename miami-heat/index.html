Services and prices

Prices checked September 2026

NBA League Pass
League Pass Premium is $24.99 a month and adds three concurrent devices, commercial-free in-arena streams and offline viewing. Season-long billing is also sold and runs through the end of the regular season. Out-of-market games only: national and local broadcasts are blacked out live. Buying League Pass on NBA.com also includes NBA TV at no extra cost. Sold on NBA.com and through Amazon, which is its exclusive channel store. It is no longer available on YouTube TV or YouTube Primetime Channels.
$16.99/mo
DirecTV
Choice plan, which is the cheapest tier carrying regional sports networks and therefore the relevant one for local NBA games. Entertainment is $89.99 without RSNs, Ultimate is $124.99, and a pared-down Sports + News plan is $55.99. Regional sports fees may apply.
$94.99/mo
Fubo Elite
Elite plan: everything in Pro plus about 70 more channels, NBA TV among them. Price not confirmed: after the July 2026 increase 2026 sources give $98.99, $99, $89.99 and $88.99. A regional sports fee is added in markets with regional sports networks. Does not carry the Warner-owned channels.
Price not confirmed
YouTube TV
Base plan, 100+ channels, unlimited DVR, three simultaneous streams. ESPN Unlimited is unlocked in the ESPN app for subscribers.
$82.99/mo
YouTube TV Sports Plan
New users pay $44.99 a month for the first 6 months if they sign up by September 30, 2026. Includes local channels and sports networks; lineup varies by market.
$64.99/mo
Fubo Pro
Pro plan. $88.99 a month since the July 2026 increase that followed NBC's return. A regional sports fee is added in markets with regional sports networks and is not optional. Does not carry the Warner-owned channels.
$88.99/mo
Hulu + Live TV
With ads, after a 3-day free trial. Price for new subscribers as of September 23, 2026; anyone who signed up earlier moves to it on their first bill on or after October 21, 2026. Includes Disney+ and ESPN.
$99.99/mo
Sling TV (Orange + Blue)
Orange alone is $45.99 and carries the ESPN family. Blue alone is $45.99 and carries NBC in some markets. Local channel coverage is limited, so many subscribers pair it with an antenna.
$60.99/mo
ESPN Unlimited
Raised from $29.99 on September 17, 2026. Annual plan $319.99. Includes ESPN on ABC. The Disney+, Hulu and ESPN Unlimited bundle is $35.99 a month and stayed at that price through the increase.
$31.99/mo
Peacock Premium
Raised from $10.99 on August 18, 2026. Annual plan $129.99. The cheaper Select tier at $8.99 carries no live sports and does not work for NBA games. Premium Plus is $19.99. Every NBA game on NBC also streams live on Peacock, and Peacock has exclusive Monday games of its own.
$12.99/mo
Prime Video
An Amazon Prime membership is $14.99 a month or $139 a year and includes NBA games on Prime Video. A standalone Prime Video subscription is $8.99 a month; check whether live NBA games are included before buying it for that reason alone.
$14.99/mo
